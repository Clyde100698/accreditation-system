## 💡 Production & Recording Checklist for Video Creators

1. **Voiceover Delivery:**
   - Record with a USB condenser mic or good headset in a quiet room.
   - Maintain an upbeat, confident, tech-product announcer tone (think Apple Keynote or SaaS commercial).
2. **Screen Recording Transitions:**
   - Record at **1080p 60fps** (1920x1080) with a clean browser window (hide bookmarks bar, press `F11` for full screen).
   - Use subtle cursor zooms when showing the mathematical formula and the What-If simulation delta.
3. **Background Music:**
   - Use royalty-free modern corporate tech music (e.g., from YouTube Audio Library or Envato). Keep music volume at **-20dB to -24dB** so the voice is crystal clear at **-3dB to -6dB**.
