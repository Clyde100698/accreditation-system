### INVITATION & INFORMED CONSENT

Dear Evaluator,

Thank you for participating in the formal software quality evaluation of the **Web-Based Accreditation Management and Readiness Assessment System**. This platform was engineered to streamline documentary collation, enforce separation of duties, automate PACUCOA undergraduate weighted score calculations (summing to 30), provide What-If readiness forecasting, and track post-visit compliance for academic programs at STI West Negros University.

Your honest evaluation will serve as empirical evidence validating the software quality, operational acceptability, and enterprise readiness of the system. In compliance with the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**, all responses will be handled with strict confidentiality, securely archived, and reported solely in aggregate statistical format.
